01 — EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

Where you stand

We reviewed the environmental wording GreenLeaf uses across its website, cup labels and social media. Your story is genuinely good — paper cups, FSC-certified board, a real industrial-compost option — but the wording overstates it in ways that become a liability under the EU’s ECGT Directive from 27 September 2026, and one claim (“plastic-free”) appears to be factually incorrect given the PLA lining.

02 — SCOPE & METHOD

What we checked

Every environmental or “green” claim on your public channels was identified, rated against the ECGT and related packaging rules (PPWR), and matched to the evidence that would substantiate it. For each, we give a compliant rewrite and the specific proof to hold on file.

This review covers: claims wording and the evidence path. It does not provide: legal sign-off, certification, or a life-cycle assessment. Where a claim is high-stakes, we flag it for a lawyer or accredited body — see §6.

04 — REWRITES (BEFORE → AFTER)

Your copy, made compliant

Same warmth, but specific and provable. Drop-in replacements for your product page and label.

Before

“The eco-friendly choice — 100% compostable and biodegradable, completely plastic-free and chemical-free, kind to the planet and carbon neutral.”

After

“Made with FSC-certified paper and a plant-based PLA lining. The cup body is certified industrially compostable to EN 13432 (where industrial composting is available). Free from added BPA and PFAS.”

Before

“We make the most sustainable cup on the market — fully recyclable, plant-based, committed to a zero-waste future.”

After

“A lower-impact cup: renewable paper board and a plant-based lining. Recyclable where local facilities accept lined paper cups. Our goal: 30% less material per cup by 2027 (progress reported yearly).”

Note: the “after” wording assumes the evidence in §5 is confirmed. Anything not yet evidenced is written conservatively or held back until proof is in hand.

06 — RECOMMENDED “PROOF” PAGE

Turn compliance into a selling point

Add one page — “Our materials & claims” — that lists each claim beside its evidence (FSC number, EN 13432 scope, lining spec). It does three things: satisfies the new rules, reassures the cafés and distributors who now ask, and — written for Google & AI search — helps the right buyers find you. We provide the page copy and the search-optimisation (AEO) as part of delivery.

⚠️ Scope & limitations. This dossier is claims-hygiene and evidence guidance, not legal advice or certification. It flags risk patterns under Directive (EU) 2024/825 (ECGT), the PPWR (EU) 2025/40 and related rules, and identifies the standards typically used to substantiate claims. For high-stakes or contested claims, confirm with a qualified lawyer or accredited certification body before publishing.
