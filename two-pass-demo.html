    Beyond schema — other ways to be found

    • Answer the buyer’s exact question in text. A short “Is this cup compostable? Yes — certified EN 13432…” Q&A (with FAQ schema) is what answer engines lift.
    • Link out to the certifier / official registry. A link to your FSC or Vakinn listing (plus sameAs) lets a machine confirm you’re real — the entity-trust signal.
    • One canonical “Our materials & claims” page. One stable URL per claim beats the same words scattered across the site.
    • Put claims in text, never baked into product images. A claim inside a photo is invisible to crawlers.
    • Be specific. Vague claims aren’t cited and aren’t compliant — specificity serves both.
    • Claim your entity. Google Business Profile + consistent name/details across the web help AI recognise the brand.
